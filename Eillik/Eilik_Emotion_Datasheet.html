Eilik Emotion Datasheet

Every way to make the robot show an expression — by touch, by motion, and on its own. Watch the round face; the serial monitor (115200) mirrors each one as emotion N/38 NAME.

38 expressions
3 touch surfaces
hold 5–10 s, then Smile
  • One finger
    Pads are tuned for a single fingertip through the shell — no need to press hard.
  • It lingers
    Each reaction holds a random 5–10 s after you let go, then returns to Smile.
  • Newest wins
    A fresh touch interrupts the current emotion and its sound — the latest input takes over.
  • It has a life
    Left alone it fidgets, then winds down to sleep. Any touch wakes it.

Head pad — top of the head

Tap patterns GPIO 32

Counter resets after 10 s untouched.

One tap→06Playful Wink
Two quick taps (<0.4 s apart)→26Playful
Tap 4× within 10 s (over-patting)→37Embarrassed

Chest pad — belly / back GPIO 14

The reaction is chosen on release, by how long you held it.

Quick tap (<0.25 s)→02Giddy
Two quick taps (<0.4 s)→26Playful
Hold 0.25–0.8 s→10Anticipation
Hold 0.8–2 s→04Charmed
Hold >2 s (long hug)→30Loving

Screen poke — tap the round display

The annoyance ladder

Keep poking and it gets crankier. Chain resets after 8 s.

1st poke→28Cheeky Tongue
2nd→21Questioning
3rd – 4th→19Annoyed
5th→16Grumpy
6th→17Furious
7th and beyond→18Fuming

Two-sensor combos

These override single-pad reactions. Hold one, tap the other.

Hold chest, tap head→03Ecstatic
Hold head, tap chest→03Ecstatic
Touch head + tap screen (<0.8 s)→27Mischievous
Touch chest + tap screen (<0.8 s)→20Flushed

Motion — shake it, knock the desk

Shake & knock

Shake reactions escalate if repeated within 12 s. Let a gesture finish first — it briefly ignores its own movement.

Shake — 1st time→24Confused Spiral
Shake again (<12 s)→35Scared
Shake a 3rd time (<12 s)→13Crying Softly
Knock the desk beside it→25Wonder

On its own — no input needed

Idle life & sleep

Leave it completely alone and it runs this by itself.

Every 7–16 s idle→a random fidget
After ~35 s idle→wind-down begins
Bored / Thoughtful / Pout → Tired / Sleepy → Yawning→32Sleeping Zzz
Touch it while asleep→01Smile
